About the Event
What is TechSpark Okitipupa?

A one-day technology and innovation conference for secondary school students and teachers in Okitipupa — designed to ignite curiosity, spark ideas, and open doors.

🚀

Inspiring Talks

Learn from industry professionals in tech, design, and cybersecurity who started just like you.

🛠️

Hands-On Breakouts

Join interactive sessions on coding, app building, and digital skills you can start using today.

🤝

Panel Discussion

Ask real questions to real professionals — how they got started and where technology is headed.

🏆

Prizes & Certificates

Walk away with more than knowledge — win prizes and earn a certificate of participation.

Barr. Omotoyosi Edeboh

Legal Practitioner & Technology Advocate
🎤 Keynote Speaker

About

Barr. Omotoyosi Edeboh is a legal practitioner and passionate technology advocate dedicated to empowering young people across Nigeria through digital literacy and innovation. She brings together her expertise in law and her belief in the transformative power of technology to inspire the next generation of problem-solvers and creators.

Talk Synopsis

"Beyond the Classroom – Unlocking Youth Potential Through Technology"

In this keynote, Barr. Edeboh challenges students to see technology not just as a tool, but as a language — one that opens doors to careers, businesses, and solutions that can transform communities. She will share how the digital economy is reshaping opportunity in Nigeria and what that means for every young person in the room today.

🎨

Kayode Owoyale

Brand Designer & Creative Entrepreneur
🎨 Talk 1

About

Kayode Owoyale is a brand designer and digital creative professional whose work sits at the intersection of creativity, technology, communication, and business strategy. He is passionate about helping people — especially young people — understand how creative thinking and digital skills can be transformed into practical solutions and meaningful opportunities.

Talk Synopsis

"Design is a Superpower: How Creativity Builds Tech Products"

Kayode introduces students to the world of UI/UX design and branding — showing that the way apps and websites look and feel is a career path, not an accident. Using real before-and-after examples, he demonstrates how creativity plus problem-solving equals a powerful tech skill that anyone can develop, even with limited resources.

🔐

Omosola Omolola (Ruth)

CS Graduate & Cybersecurity Tutor, FUTA
🔐 Talk 2

About

Omosola Omolola, known as Ruth, is a Computer Science graduate of Adeleke University currently pursuing her Master's degree at the Federal University of Technology, Akure (FUTA). She has hands-on experience tutoring students in Python and cybersecurity, and her research interests include healthcare data privacy, AI, and blockchain applications in healthcare systems.

Talk Synopsis

"Stay Safe, Stay Smart: An Introduction to Cybersecurity & Your Digital Life"

Ruth uses real examples that students already encounter — WhatsApp scams, fake links, data breaches — to explain what cybersecurity means and why it matters right now. She closes with simple, actionable habits every student can adopt today to protect themselves online.
